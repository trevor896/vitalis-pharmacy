BEFORE YOU SEND

Help us review your prescription accurately

A few simple checks can help the pharmacy team understand your request and contact you if anything needs clarification.

Make the image easy to read

Use good lighting, keep the full page in frame, and check that names, strengths, directions, and dates are not blurred or covered.

Include useful context

Mention allergies, current medicines, refill timing, or a generic preference in the notes. Never leave out information that could affect safe dispensing.

Keep your original prescription

An image helps start a request but may not replace an original or valid prescription. Keep it available and follow the pharmacist's instructions.

Important: Prescription requirements vary by medicine. Some medicines may require additional checks, an original prescription, or an in-person consultation. The pharmacy will confirm what is needed.
